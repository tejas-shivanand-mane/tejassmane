Tejas Mane

My research focuses on fault-tolerant distributed systems — consensus protocols, geo-replication, and real-time scheduling.

I'm a Computer Science PhD candidate at UC Riverside, advised by Prof. Mohsen Lesani in the Distributed Systems Lab. My research spans Byzantine-fault-tolerant geo-replication (HAMAVA, ICDE 2025), failure recovery in distributed task systems, unauthenticated Byzantine consensus, and causal scheduling for cyber-physical systems.

Before distributed systems, I built machine-learning and computer-vision pipelines for clinical imaging and 3D graphics, with publications across systems, vision, and computational materials science.
